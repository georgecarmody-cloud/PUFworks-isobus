MODULE: PUFworks-isobus
ROLE: ISO 11783 / J1939 bus engine & safety gate
STATUS: active

ONE-LINER
Listens to vision section intent and decides what (if anything) may be transmitted on the sprayer CAN bus, under a strict authority ladder.

SYNOPSIS
- Consumes SectionBitmapV1 from PUFworks-vision (VISION_BITMAP: stdin); no camera, OpenCV, or YOLO in this repo.
- Boots in OBSERVE (zero CAN TX); operator must climb Control Authority and ARM before section or rate commands go on the wire.
- Fail-safes: >300 ms without fresh vision → all sections closed; no UI heartbeat >3 s while armed → demote and disarm; speed <0.5 km/h → sections closed.
- Platform paths (closed decisions, machine-specific, not AEF-certified): GRC-class DDI 141 section TX; 616R = GreenSeeker serial + whole-boom blanking only — no CAN section injection.
- Not a drop-in sprayer ECU or Virtual Terminal; not affiliated with AEF, John Deere, or Goldacres. Boots listen-only (OBSERVE).
- Also provides spray PGN decode library (catalog + element decodes at https://pufworks.farm/decode/), hardware guide at https://pufworks.farm/isobus/hardware/, session recorder, 616R field sniff tools, GPS/CAN WiFi hub, and bench harness for virtual-bus smoke tests.
- Does NOT run vision inference, agronomy capture, or direct solenoid GPIO (use PUFworks-actuation for non-ISOBUS hardware).
